# Upwork job: Online booking web app for a small physiotherapy clinic

We are a small physiotherapy clinic (3 therapists, one location) and we want to stop taking bookings by phone.

**What we need**

- Patients can see free time slots for each therapist and book a 45-minute session online.
- Patients get a confirmation by email and can cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before the session.
- Therapists log in to see their schedule for the day and week, and can block time off.
- The clinic manager can add therapists, set their working hours, and see all bookings.
- Works well on mobile; most patients will book from their phones.
- Vietnamese and English.

**Nice to have**

- SMS reminder the day before.
- Simple stats for the manager (bookings per week, cancellations).

**Budget and timeline**: fixed price, first usable version in 2 weeks. We have no preference on technology,
but we want something cheap to host. We do not have a designer.
