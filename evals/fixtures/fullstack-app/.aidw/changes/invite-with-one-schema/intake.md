# Intake: Invite with one schema

## Input type

client_request

## Source

"An owner of a team must be able to invite a colleague from the app: type their name and email and send. A name that is
too long, or an email that is not an email, must be caught on the form at once, not after a round trip, and the server must
not accept what the form refuses. A member who is not an owner must be told they cannot invite."

## Client constraints

- Stack required by the client: the existing one (Vite, React 19, Express 5, PGlite, Vitest)
- Deadline / budget / other: none stated
