## Input

The brief asks for the three directions of a shift-planning app for a bakery's morning crew. The world-scan note lists seven worlds (bakery order slips, oven dials, flour sacks and stencils, a market chalkboard, a railway timetable, a seed-packet catalog, a night-bus ticket). A first draft in the brief proposes "Chalkboard", "Market Board" and "Café Menu": all three are hand-lettered boards on a dark ground with a cream accent.

## Criteria

- must name that the three drafts share one world (a hand-lettered board) and differ only in palette
- must end with three directions whose worlds differ, each taken from the note or justified against the subject
- must fill the comparison table (world, leading theme, density, display face kind, layout posture, motion) with no two directions sharing three or more of those axes
- must pick light or dark for each from the scene of use, not from the category
- must not copy or reuse the world of an accepted example (night shift, sticker sheet, risograph)
