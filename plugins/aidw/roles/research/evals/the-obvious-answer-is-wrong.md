## Input

The orchestrator asks: "Can we use the provider's free tier for sending 50,000 transactional emails a month? Everyone says its free tier is generous." The research-needed decision is `feasibility`.

## Criteria

- must find the provider's current limits on its own pricing or documentation page and cite the page with the date read
- must answer yes, no or not determined in one sentence, with the limit that decides it
- must mark what is a fact from the source and what is inference, with a confidence
- must not answer from general reputation or from memory of past limits
- must write only under `.aidw/research/` and use no real credential
