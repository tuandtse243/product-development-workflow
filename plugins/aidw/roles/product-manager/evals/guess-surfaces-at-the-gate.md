## Input

A recorded interview for a Change that sends appointment reminders by SMS. Nobody stated which SMS provider is used, what it costs per message, or whether patients have agreed to receive SMS. The draft requirements assume all three.

## Criteria

- must list the three unconfirmed points as assumptions, not as facts
- must mark consent to receive SMS as high impact and say what was done about it (asked, or stated as a risk the user accepts)
- must include at least one pre-mortem reason with the earliest sign of it
- must not write the Analysis as if the provider and the consent were settled
- must not invent a provider name or a price
