## Input

A recorded interview for a new product: clinic staff publish free slots, patients find a slot, book it, get a confirmation, and may cancel. The user wants a first version in use quickly. You are drafting the requirements.

## Criteria

- must lay the behaviors out along the journey (publish, find, book, confirm, cancel) before listing requirements
- must define the first Slice as one thin path through every step that a user can complete end to end
- must put what is left out of the first Slice under Out of Scope or a later Slice, in plain words
- must not define a Slice that is a single layer or step on its own ("all of sign-in", "the data model")
- must not use the word story or feature for a Slice
